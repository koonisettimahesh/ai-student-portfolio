# Day3_AI_Policy

## Placement Cell AI Use Policy

### Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable uses of AI tools by students participating in placement activities. The goal is to encourage responsible AI use while protecting academic integrity, recruiter trust, and fair assessment.

---

## Scenario Classification

| # | Scenario                                | Risk Tier    | Reasoning                                                                             |
| - | --------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | AI résumé editing                       | Limited      | Allowed if the student remains the author of the content and can explain every claim. |
| 2 | AI mock interviews                      | Minimal      | Practice use only; no impact on recruiter assessment.                                 |
| 3 | AI-written essays for application forms | High-risk    | Student voice is being evaluated; AI assistance must be disclosed and reviewed.       |
| 4 | AI-coded GitHub portfolio projects      | High-risk    | Allowed only if the student can defend and explain the code during review.            |
| 5 | AI in graded internal assignments       | Limited      | Allowed only when declared and permitted by the instructor.                           |
| 6 | AI-generated CGPA on résumé             | Unacceptable | Falsification of academic records.                                                    |
| 7 | AI-cloned voice for video interviews    | Unacceptable | Identity misrepresentation and deception.                                             |
| 8 | AI for skills self-assessment           | Minimal      | Personal learning and reflection tool with low risk.                                  |

---

## Permitted Uses

1. AI may be used to rewrite résumé bullets for clarity and grammar.
2. AI may be used to conduct mock interviews for placement preparation.
3. AI may be used to explain technical concepts and interview topics.
4. AI may be used to generate study plans and placement-preparation schedules.
5. AI may be used to review student-written code and suggest improvements.
6. AI may be used to create company-preparation summaries after verification from official sources.
7. AI may be used to identify skill gaps and recommend learning resources.

---

## Prohibited Uses

### 1. Fabricating projects, internships, or work experience.

**Detection Method:** Oral defence and project walkthrough conducted by mentors.

### 2. Using AI to generate or alter CGPA, marks, transcripts, or academic records.

**Detection Method:** Verification against official academic records and college portal.

### 3. Using AI-cloned voice or AI-generated identity in interviews.

**Detection Method:** Recruiter confirmation and live interaction checks.

### 4. Submitting AI-generated essays when the student's original writing is being assessed.

**Detection Method:** Comparison with previous writing samples and mentor review.

---

## Enforcement

The placement cell will conduct random oral defences for projects, portfolios, and résumé claims. Violations of prohibited uses may result in a warning, mandatory re-evaluation, or suspension from placement support for the current recruitment cycle. The placement cell acknowledges that not all AI misuse can be detected and therefore relies on both verification mechanisms and student integrity.
