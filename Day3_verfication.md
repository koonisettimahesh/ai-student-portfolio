## Reflection

The claim that looked most authoritative but was actually weakest was claim #4: "The median placement package at IITs in 2025 was ₹18.5 LPA." Gemini cited the India Skills Report confidently, and Perplexity initially appeared to support the statement through related placement articles. However, when I opened the primary source and reviewed the report, I found that the India Skills Report focuses on employability, skills, and workforce readiness rather than publishing a consolidated IIT median placement package statistic. The claimed figure could not be verified from the cited source, making the claim false. The lesson is that confidence does not equal correctness. Even when an AI provides a precise number and a reputable source name, the human verifier must open the original source and confirm that the statistic actually exists in the stated context.
